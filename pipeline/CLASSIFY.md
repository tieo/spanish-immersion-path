# Immersion or lesson

Every listed video is classified from its title (and channel) as **immersion** or
**lesson**, by a language model following these rules; the result is data/classes.json,
one `{"id", "kind", "why"}` per video. Only immersion videos enter the playlist.

**Immersion:** Spanish spoken by native speakers: real or slow conversations, street
interviews, vlogs, city tours, stories, podcasts in Spanish, cooking, routines, games and
challenges played in Spanish, culture or history told in Spanish. A street interview that
asks people about a language topic (slang, words they hate) is immersion: it is native
speakers talking.

**Lesson (left out):** videos whose purpose is explaining: grammar, conjugation, tenses,
"X vs Y", "how to say X", pronunciation lessons, lists of words or phrases to memorise,
learner mistakes; videos spoken mainly in English (advice on learning, method talks,
Q&A); channel announcements and promotions; music, ASMR or sleep videos without much
speech; anything under two minutes.

Dreaming Spanish titles its Spanish videos in English, often with a level word
(Superbeginner, Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced); those are immersion. Titles about
learning methods, progress or hours are usually English talks.

An English video that slips through is caught by the captions: YouTube only produces the
Spanish speech-recognition track for videos spoken in Spanish.
